For yield strength the Simulator uses, in order: 1) a library grade's datasheet value if your mix matches one, 2) the steel model (312 measured steels) for alloyed steels inside its range, 3) the library model (48 grades + the 312 steels + 456 aluminium measurements) inside its range, 4) otherwise no ML estimate, only a rough formula marked low reliability.
