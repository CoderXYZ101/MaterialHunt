Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

MaterialHunt is an independent project based in Bengaluru, India. This page explains in plain words what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can delete it. We try to follow the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who this is for, including young people

MaterialHunt is a free tool for engineers, makers, students and buyers. If you are under 18, you may create an account only with the permission of a parent or guardian. You confirm this when you sign up. We do not show advertisements, and we do not track anyone for advertising.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, your name or organisation, the role you pick, and a password (kept by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot read it). If you use Google sign-in, Google tells us your name and email.
  • Optional profile: display name, a short bio, organisation, and an optional small photo. You choose whether your photo shows on the leaderboard and whether other members can find you by name.
  • Things you create: saved designs, leaderboard entries (your display name, alloy mix and score are public), community challenges, endorsements, feedback, quote (RFQ) and upgrade requests.
  • Usage analytics: only if you accept the cookie notice. They tell us which tools are used, and contain no advertising identifiers.
  • On your device: settings and your chat history with the assistant are kept in your browser's storage.

How we use it

  • To run your account, send verification and support emails, and show your saved work.
  • To run the weekly leaderboard, prizes and community features.
  • To keep the service safe (for example, reviewing reported profiles and photos) and to improve it.

The AI assistant and photos

  • Many answers and all quick photo checks run on your device.
  • For open-ended questions, your question and a short summary of your current simulation are sent to an AI service (Cloudflare Workers AI, and possibly other AI providers we may configure) to produce an answer. Do not type personal details, passwords or confidential information into the assistant.
  • A photo leaves your device only if you press "Escalate to Cloud AI". It is used to produce that one answer.

Who can see your data

  • We do not sell your data and do not share it for marketing.
  • Other signed-in members can see your display name, organisation, role choice and bio if you create a profile. Your photo is visible to others only if you switch that on. Leaderboard entries are public.
  • Requests you send for quotes may be passed on to the supplier or lab needed to answer them.
  • Our service providers process data for us: Google (Firebase hosting, sign-in and database) and Cloudflare (AI answers).

Deleting your data

You can delete your data yourself at any time: open your Profile page and use Delete my profile or Delete my account. Deleting your account removes your account, profile, saved designs, leaderboard and community entries, requests, feedback and endorsement. A few records that are not personal stay (for example public counters and prize records already paid). You can also ask us to delete or export your data using the Contact link below.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. Inactive data may be cleaned up from time to time.

Security

Data is stored with Google Firebase over encrypted connections, and the database has rules so members can only read and change their own private data. No online service is perfectly secure, so use a strong, unique password.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Questions, requests or concerns: use the Contact link on the website (the feedback button).
